## 📥 **Quick Image Integration Steps**

### Step 1: Download Images
Visit: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/home-healthcare

Download images showing:
- ✅ Caregiver with elderly person at home
- ✅ Person in wheelchair at home
- ✅ Healthcare worker portrait (professional, friendly)

### Step 2: Prepare Images
- Rename to match required filenames
- Optimize using https://tinypng.com
- Target size: 500KB or less per image

### Step 3: Upload
Save to: `c:\Users\USER\mmabb\public\images\`

### Step 4: Verify
1. Refresh website at http://127.0.0.1:8000
2. Check homepage hero section
3. Images should load automatically
4. Fallback icons will disappear when images are present

## 🆘 **Need Help Finding Images?**

### Best Free Sources:
1. **Unsplash** - https://unsplash.com
   - Highest quality
   - Best for professional photos
   - Free commercial license

2. **Pexels** - https://www.pexels.com
   - Good variety
   - Easy search
   - Free commercial license

3. **Pixabay** - https://pixabay.com
   - Large library
   - Multiple categories
   - Free commercial license

### Search Tips:
- "home healthcare"
- "elderly care at home"
- "caregiver with senior"
- "wheelchair accessible home"
- "disability support residential"

Remember: **HOME settings, not HOSPITAL!**
