## 🎨 Image Style Guidelines

### Color & Tone
- **Warm, natural tones** (avoid cold/clinical blues)
- **Soft, natural lighting** (not harsh fluorescent)
- **Professional but approachable**
- **High quality** (no pixelation or artifacts)

### Composition
- **Focus on people and relationships**
- **Clear, uncluttered backgrounds**
- **Good depth of field** (professional photography)
- **Horizontal orientation** (landscape) preferred

### Subjects
- **Diverse representation** (age, ethnicity, ability)
- **Authentic interactions** (not overly posed)
- **Respectful depiction** of people with disabilities
- **Professional caregivers** in appropriate attire

## 🚫 What to Avoid

### Settings
❌ Hospital wards
❌ Clinical examination rooms
❌ GP surgeries
❌ Operating theatres
❌ Ambulances
❌ Emergency departments

### People/Props
❌ Doctors in white coats
❌ Stethoscopes around necks
❌ Surgical masks/caps
❌ Lab coats
❌ Medical charts/clipboards in clinical settings
❌ IV poles (unless in home setting)

### Scenarios
❌ Medical examinations
❌ Blood pressure checks in clinical settings
❌ Injection/medication administration (too clinical)
❌ Surgical procedures
❌ Emergency scenarios

## 📥 How to Download & Add Images

### Step 1: Find Images
Visit these FREE stock photo sites:
1. **Unsplash** - https://unsplash.com
   - Search: "home healthcare", "elderly care", "disability support"
   - License: Free for commercial use

2. **Pexels** - https://www.pexels.com
   - Search: "home care", "caregiver", "elderly home"
   - License: Free for commercial use

3. **Pixabay** - https://pixabay.com
   - Search: "homecare", "caregiver elderly", "nursing home"
   - License: Free for commercial use

### Step 2: Download Images
- Choose high-resolution versions (at least 1920px wide)
- Download as JPG format
- Rename according to the filename guide above

### Step 3: Optimize Images
Before uploading, optimize for web:
- Use online tool: https://tinypng.com or https://squoosh.app
- Target file size: Under 500KB per image
- Maintain quality while reducing file size

### Step 4: Upload to Website
1. Save images to: `c:\Users\USER\mmabb\public\images\`
2. Use the exact filenames listed in this guide
3. Refresh the website to see changes

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Image Selection
1. **Choose authentic moments** over posed studio shots
2. **Prioritize home settings** that look like real homes
3. **Show dignity and respect** in all images
4. **Avoid overly medicalized** imagery
5. **Ensure diversity** in age, ethnicity, gender

### Quality Checks
- ✅ Image is sharp and well-lit
- ✅ Subjects look natural and comfortable
- ✅ Setting is appropriate (home/community)
- ✅ No distracting backgrounds
- ✅ Professional but warm tone

### Accessibility
- All images should have descriptive alt text
- Images should support (not replace) text content
- Consider users with visual impairments
