## 📝 Content Guidelines for Images

When adding images to the site, follow these guidelines:

### ✅ USE Images Showing:
- Adults receiving complex care at home
- Children with complex needs in community settings
- Caregivers supporting clients in home environments
- Home healthcare settings (living rooms, bedrooms adapted for care)
- Disability support in community
- Community healthcare activities
- Family members with care recipients

### ❌ AVOID Images Showing:
- Doctors wearing stethoscopes
- Hospital or clinical settings
- GP surgery environments
- White coats or clinical uniforms
- Medical equipment in hospital contexts
- Stock "doctor and patient" photos
- Generic medical imagery

### Image Placement Opportunities:
1. **Homepage hero** - Replace placeholder with home care scene
2. **About Us page** - Leadership team photo
3. **Services section** - Complex care delivery at home
4. **Case studies** - Real outcomes (with consent)
